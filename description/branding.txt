An artisanal small-batch ice cream brand.
 A holistic, environment friendly, product, delicious and healthy and is a fun rainbow fusion of Indian flavours and mythology, traditional Italian flavours and exotic flavours from around the world.
mission, 
As yogis we are concerned about the environment and the health and quality of what we eat., at the same time Human society is a bouquet of beautiful different colours and cultures and we like our ice cream to express that beauty and diversity in a creative way..
Samadhi Eis is also dedicated to support service projects which improve the conditions of plants, animals and human beings in distress.

objectives, 
Become a popular Alternative artisanal ice cream brand in urban markets and specialty stores within 3 years.
Build a loyal community through Instagram and social media
Achieve 30%+ year-over-year growth by emphasizing sustainability (eco-friendly packaging, local sourcing). Creative genius and originality
Differentiate from mass-market brands by focusing on creative storytelling, natural ingredients, and emotional connection.
target audience... 
Primary: Women and men aged 25–55, urban or suburban, with higher disposable income.
•	They value quality, aesthetics, wellness (natural/plant-based options), and shareable experiences (Instagram, TikTok).
•	Secondary: Families with kids (fun flavors + cute visuals) and foodies seeking unique, photogenic desserts.
•	Psychographics: Creative, mindful consumers who enjoy "treat yourself" moments and seasonal trends.
