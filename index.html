Our Story

1986

The Seed is Planted

After learning meditation and becoming vegetarian, I had my first food industry experience with a hot dog cart in Johannesburg. It was a humbling start that taught me the real world doesn't always follow our dreams.

Italy

The Art of Gelato

Living in Italy for several years, I immersed myself in the cuisine and discovered the true art of ice cream making.

2017

Samadhi Eis is Born

In Wendelsheim, Germany, we transformed an old bakery into our first kitchen. Our first festival was a "mud bath" disaster – broken machines, power outages, and rain. It was a rock-bottom moment, but as yogis, we learned from the obstacles.

"They teach us as long as we want to learn from them."

Innovation

A New Recipe

Restless to leave the beaten track, I spent the winter experimenting. Through trial and error, I developed our signature base using Cashew nuts and Coconut milk. By Summer 2018, we had 16 unique vegan flavors.

2019-2022

Growth & Resilience

We moved to Hanover, then faced the challenges of Covid. After a brief stint in Berlin's Friedrichshain, we decided to return to our roots in Wendelsheim in 2022.

Today

The Ingredient of Love

Today, we are regular participants in festivals across Germany. As a yogic monk, the special attribute I bring is the "altruistic spirit." We produce ice cream not just for profit, but to communicate tolerance, respect for the environment, and a profound love for what we do.

Social Engagement

Founder

Dada Devavratananda

Acharya, Monk, and Teacher of Yoga and Meditation

In the tradition of Ananda Marga Yoga with over 35 years of experience. Originally from Africa, after becoming a monk, Dada Devavratananda dedicated his entire life to traveling and living all over the world with the purpose of teaching meditation, yoga, and spreading Neohumanism. He currently teaches meditation and engages in charitable projects in Germany and Ukraine.

"Dada is the embodiment of yoga in practice: when it is no longer you practicing yoga, but yoga becoming your entire life, the path to perfection permeates your entire character and spirit. And this is yoga in its deepest sense—self-realization and service to all creation!"
