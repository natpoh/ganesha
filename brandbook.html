Identity Guidelines

The Soul of Samadhi

Visual logic, philosophy, and aesthetic parameters for "The Yoga of Ice Cream".

1. Brand Philosophy

Mission

"To nourish body and soul with divine plant-based treats that honor the planet and uplift the spirit."

Brand Slogan

"Immerse yourself in the colors of divine bliss."

Our Values (The Mantra)

  • 🕊️ Ahimsa (Non-violence): 100% Vegan. True pleasure shouldn't cause harm.
  • 🌱 Sattva (Purity): Only natural ingredients. Pure energy for a pure mind.
  • ✨ Ananda (Bliss): Food is a celebration. Vibrant, unexpected flavors.

Tone of Voice: "Enlightened Playfulness"

We communicate like a wise but fun friend. We use spiritual references lightly and with a smile, never preachy.

✅ DO "Send your taste buds an invite to nirvana."
❌ DON'T "We manufacture a high-quality lactose-free product."

1.1 Target Audience

Profile: "The Conscious Hedonist"

Demographics
  • Age: 20–40 (Gen Z & Millennials).
  • Location: Major cities, urbanites.
  • Income: Middle+, willing to pay for quality and ethical sourcing.
Psychographics
  • Cares about health but won't sacrifice taste.
  • Interested in yoga, meditation, self-growth, or "slow living".
  • Eco-activist at heart (recycles, chooses cruelty-free).
  • Loves travel and exotic flavor experiences.

2. Logo

The "Ganesha G" Icon. A warm, approachable 'G' icon featuring a playful, young Ganesha. The design is inviting and joyous, perfectly capturing the "Enlightened Playfulness" of the brand.

Psychological Harmony (Pastel Tetradic)

Warm Tones (Orange #FF9933, Yellow #FFD659, Pink #E5A3AE): Create an inviting, appetizing, analogous flow (The Hedonistic Ice Cream experience).

Cool/Neutral Tones (Purple #CDB4DB, Green #DCE2AC): Act as perfect color-wheel opposites to the warm tones, adding natural grounding and spiritual calm (The Yogic Conscious experience).

4. Typography

Concept: The Dance of Tradition & Modernity

We pair Ramaraja, a highly readable serif with classic Indian modulation, with Outfit, a clean geometric sans-serif that speaks to modern vegan purity. This balances the brand's soulful heritage with accessible communication.

Headings

Primary Font

Ramaraja

Used for H1, H2, and logos. Provides the distinct feel of Indian calligraphy but with stronger, more uniform strokes.
